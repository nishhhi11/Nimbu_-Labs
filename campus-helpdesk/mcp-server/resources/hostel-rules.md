# Nimbus Campus Hostel Rules & Guidelines

## 1. Gate Timings & Curfew
- **Main Hostel Gate Closing Time**: 22:00 (10:00 PM) on weekdays (Sunday to Thursday).
- **Weekend Gate Closing Time**: 23:00 (11:00 PM) on Friday and Saturday.
- Late entries without prior written approval from the Warden will incur disciplinary action.

## 2. Visitor Regulations
- Visiting hours: 16:00 to 19:00 only, in designated Common Areas.
- Outside guests are strictly not permitted inside individual hostel rooms.
- Overnight stay of non-residents is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Quiet Hours & Conduct
- **Quiet Hours**: 23:00 to 06:00 every day. Playing loud music or creating disturbances during quiet hours is forbidden.
- Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or banned substances on campus premises is strictly banned and results in immediate expulsion.

## 4. Room Maintenance & Electrical Appliances
- Heavy electrical appliances (induction cookers, immersion heaters, air conditioners) are not permitted in hostel rooms.
- Residents are responsible for the cleanliness and maintenance of their allocated rooms.
